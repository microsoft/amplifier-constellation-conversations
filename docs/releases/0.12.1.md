# Conversations 0.12.1

A huddle peer's ordinary reply can now reach the participating Companion lead
after its initial turn finishes. Previously, the reply was saved in the huddle
but could leave the lead waiting unless the peer explicitly posted an outcome.

Targeted replies use the existing input queue and the lead's original session.
They retain the peer's identity and source separately from the original human
request. The lead can review the reply and publish a meaningful outcome through
the existing return path. Routine discussion and intermediate replies stay out
of automatic speech and result cards.

Human follow-ups retain their existing steering behavior. Duplicate posts do
not dispatch another turn, uncertain delivery is not retried, and Stop,
revocation and changed session bindings remain effective. Peer input cannot
grant new human-directed actions or borrow the caller's display controls.

Later clarifications keep their own saved source while continuing the same
discussion. Requested outcomes return through the spoken-result channel with
the matching caller request. Complete reports, including qualifications, use
the existing ordered delivery path; longer reports retain every character
across bounded fragments. Saved cards keep the full report and its attribution.
Newer caller input can move an earlier answer into quiet context.
During a call, the huddle panel scrolls so longer messages and the Join controls
remain reachable without moving the call controls.

Successful tool-only or silent turns now retain their completed input and
response records. They do not require extra public text, create empty result
cards, or block a later human question. Actual errors and uncertain receipts
remain distinct and are not automatically retried.

No new ACP extension, scheduler, model setting or connector service is required.
External participants continue using their own delivery loops. Existing huddle
configuration is preserved; see [huddle delivery](../huddles.md).
