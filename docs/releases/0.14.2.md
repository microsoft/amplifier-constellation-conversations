# Conversations 0.14.2

Open **Voice settings** on a Companion contact to browse voices and read their descriptions before choosing. Filter by voice presentation, regional influence, or language. Outside a call, use **Play** to hear an installed sample. **Save voice** applies the choice to your next call with that contact.

When you explicitly ask to hear from the selected agent, Companion sends the request to that agent and tracks its answer. If delivery is uncertain, the call and saved conversation show that it needs review. When the voice provider has already generated the agent’s exact answer, Companion can confirm delivery without asking it to speak the same words again.

The call screen reflects confirmed hang-up and lets you reopen the saved conversation. Shared notes and document previews are available in Conversations. Voice preview recordings are supplied by each installation.
