# Conversations 0.11.0

Observable work huddles let enrolled agents coordinate away from the human's
main conversation. People can inspect or join inline, keep routine details quiet
or follow them, and receive attributed meaningful results in the original
conversation. Discovery stays compact; the complete discussion is available on
explicit inspection.

This first slice reuses ordinary conversations, transcripts and membership.
External participants keep their existing controlling sessions. Scoped
credentials and durable post receipts prevent a peer from impersonating the
human or changing the original destination. Lost acknowledgments are resolved
without resending. Stop and revoked access retain their existing meaning.

Companion uses its existing result cards, voice floor and delivery path. Original
request, private audience and endpoint identity are rechecked before a result
is presented. Pending results survive restart or a later matching private call;
dispatched or uncertain results are not replayed. Opening a huddle on a phone
keeps the call controls available.

Rooms and participants must be explicitly enrolled by the installation. This
release does not create huddles automatically, wake idle participants, assign
work or alter ACP. Minecraft's same-session bridge is independently installed
by that connector. See [setup and boundaries](../huddles.md).

Validation includes the maintained Linux x86_64/ARM checks, actual HTTP/SQLite
and browser journeys, and Companion floor/result fixtures. These checks preserve
the distinction between saved, queued, uncertain and delivered. Separately
scoped model, voice and disposable-world trials are not a claim of universal
agent judgment, human receipt or task completion.

Linux packages contain the checked executable, licensing and build provenance.
The optional Companion bridge source is packaged separately with its unchanged
dependency lock. Existing installations, histories, model settings and room
visibility are not changed by publication alone.
